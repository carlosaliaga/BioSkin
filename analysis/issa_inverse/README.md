# Inverse fitting of the BioSkin decoder to ISSA facial spectra

Fits the pretrained BioSkin decoder (non-AO) to each of the 7,923 cleaned ISSA facial reflectance spectra
and scores spectra with the two-stage validation of He, Lasisi et al. (BVCM + GMM, lasisilab/SkinSim_Quarto),
used **unchanged**. Written for Section 4.3 of that paper. `REPORT.md` is the full analysis log
(feasibility → pilot → full run → specular correction). The manuscript material is regenerated by
`paper_numbers.py` and kept out of the repository until publication.

## Results

- **Reconstruction.** Median RMSE 0.87 reflectance points (3.2% of mean reflectance) over 7,923 spectra,
  with melanin, blood and thickness free.
- **Fitted melanin** falls within Jacques (1998) melanosome fractions per eumelanin class. For the darkest
  skin it reaches the 43% upper bound of the training range.
- **Measurement convention.** ISSA is d/8 with specular included (SCI); decoder output is subsurface only.
  Adding near-normal Fresnel, `R = F + (1 − F)·R_d` with F = 0.028, improves the fits. It also removes most
  of the apparent epidermal-thickness inflation, and raises the original 4,000-sample pass rate from 18.3% to 29.2%.
- **Sampling, not the model, drives forward pass rates.** The same decoder passes at 29–96% depending only
  on how its parameters are sampled.
- **Coverage.** Applied to the real ISSA spectra, their test accepts none of the 15 darkest (EH) and 71% of
  EMIH, against 94% for EMIL.

## Conventions (check before reusing any number)

- **Decoder input order.** Slot 3 is melanin type ratio (0.65–0.80) and slot 4 is blood oxygenation
  (0.90–1.00). `SKIN_PROPS` was historically mislabelled; see `bioskin/parameters/parameter.py`.
- **Parameter space.** Fits run in the unit box over the decoder's normalized inputs (melanin^⅓, blood^¼,
  thickness linear in 10–350 µm). The box is the measured min/max of the training tensor, the only bound imposed.
- **Fixed parameters.** Melanin type ratio and oxygenation are fixed at 0.725 / 0.95. They are not
  identifiable from 400–700 nm: left free, 86% of fits hit a bound. A fitted figure of 95% oxygenation
  is therefore our choice, not a result.
- **Specular.** Compare `F + (1 − F)·R_d`, F = 0.028, against ISSA. Do **not** use BioSkin's
  `lighting/light_transport.add_specular_reflectance`: it is a hemispherical average (F̄ = 0.106), meant for
  rendering under diffuse light. Its `integrate_fresnel` also double-counts sin θ; the correct average is 0.086.
- **Smoothing.** Double Savitzky–Golay (15, 3) as in the distributed packages, applied as a fixed 310×310
  linear map so it sits inside the fit. Raw output is always reported alongside, because Stage 2 penalises
  decoder roughness heavily.
- **Grid and units.** The 31 ISSA wavelengths fall exactly on the decoder grid (indices 10, 15, …, 160).
  ISSA is in percent; MI = 100·log10(100/R680), EI = 100·log10(R680/R560).
- **Their test.** Always `upstream/code/check_bioskin_rerun.evaluate(spectra_pct, reference, 2.4477)`. It
  reproduces their published 1,167 / 733 on the corrected package exactly.
- **Clustering.** Participant = ISSA origin dataset × subject number (2,095). Confidence intervals resample
  participants, not spectra.

## Data-sharing note

The Monte Carlo `.pt` spectra are internal and must not be published or used for published numbers.
`paper_numbers.py` uses only the decoder, ISSA and the training-domain bounds. The pilot's
`rmse_nearest_mc` column (`inverse_pilot.py`) reads the internal set and is an internal diagnostic only.

Outputs (`out/`, `full_out/`), the downloaded `upstream/` files and the manuscript material (`paper/`) are
not committed; see `.gitignore` in this folder. Rerun the scripts to regenerate them.
